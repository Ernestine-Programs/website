A Collective of Builders.

We are a group of friends who write software together. We share an interest in programming, new technology and the satisfaction of seeing an idea run for the first time.

We review each other’s code, try tools we have not used before, and keep what works. Some projects become products. Others are experiments that teach us something. All of them are built in the open when we can.
